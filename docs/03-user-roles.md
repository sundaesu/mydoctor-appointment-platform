# User Roles

## Patient

Patients discover doctors and clinics, book appointments, receive tokens, track queue status, and manage their own family members.

## Doctor

Doctors view today's schedule and queue, manage availability, call and complete patients, and see basic patient information.

## Receptionist

Receptionists operate the clinic workflow by registering patients, booking appointments, managing walk-ins, generating tokens, and coordinating the queue.

## Clinic Administrator

Clinic administrators are implied in the broader operating model and may eventually manage settings, staff, and clinic-level access.

## Access Notes

Role-based access is required because different users should see different information and control different workflows.
