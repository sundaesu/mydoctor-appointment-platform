# Product Vision

## Long-Term Vision

Build a scalable healthcare appointment and clinic queue platform for India, beginning with independent doctors and clinics in smaller cities and expanding city-by-city.

## Initial Geography

The first target market is India, starting with Srikakulam, Andhra Pradesh.

## Initial Problem

Private doctors, clinics, and small hospitals often manage patients with a mix of phone calls, WhatsApp, walk-ins, paper tokens, manual appointment books, and receptionist-managed queues.

## Initial Value Proposition

Help patients find doctors, book appointments and tokens, and track their clinic queue while giving doctors and receptionists simple tools to manage the real-world clinic workflow.

## Product Boundaries

The initial product is not a full healthcare ecosystem. The early focus is:

- Doctor discovery
- Appointment booking
- Token management
- Real-time queue management

## Future Possibilities

These are future capabilities, not MVP requirements:

- Digital prescriptions
- Medical records
- Laboratory integration
- Diagnostics
- Pharmacy
- Teleconsultation
- Payments
- Insurance
- Analytics
- AI-assisted healthcare workflows
