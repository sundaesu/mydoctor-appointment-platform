- Doctor availability
- Appointment booking
- Appointment confirmation
- Token generation
- Live queue status

## Explicitly Out of MVP

These are future features, not MVP:

- Full electronic medical records
- Digital prescriptions
- Pharmacy
- Laboratory integration
- Diagnostics integration
- Telemedicine
- Insurance
- Advanced billing
- AI diagnosis
- AI medical recommendations
- Complex hospital management
- National-scale integrations

## MVP Intent

The MVP should stay focused on validation of the core appointment and queue experience rather than becoming a broad healthcare suite too early.
